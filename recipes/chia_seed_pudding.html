Chia Seed Pudding

Description

This version boosts protein with Greek yogurt (or a plant-based alternative) and optional protein powder. Makes 1–2 servings.

Ingredients

  • 2–3 tbsp chia seeds (about 30–40g)
  • ¾–1 cup unsweetened almond milk (or milk of choice)
  • ½–¾ cup plain Greek yogurt (for creaminess + protein boost)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1–2 tsp maple syrup, honey, or sweetener of choice (optional)
  • Pinch of cinnamon or salt
  • Optional: ½ scoop (10–15g) vanilla protein powder for extra protein

Topping Ideas

  • Fresh berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries)
  • Handful of nuts or seeds (almonds, walnuts, hemp seeds)
  • Granola or coconut flakes

Instructions

  1. n a jar or bowl, whisk together the milk, Greek yogurt, vanilla, sweetener, and any protein powder until smooth.
  2. Stir in the chia seeds thoroughly (this prevents clumping).
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2–3 hours, or overnight. Stir once after 20–30 minutes for even texture.
  4. Top with your favorites and enjoy! It thickens into a pudding-like consistency.

Flavor Varitations

  • Chocolate: Add 1 tbsp cocoa powder + chocolate protein powder.
  • Berry: Mash in fresh or frozen berries before chilling.
  • Peanut Butter: Stir in 1 tbsp peanut butter for a savory-sweet twist.
  • Tropical: Use coconut milk + mango or pineapple on top.

Tips

  • Use a 1:4 ratio of chia seeds to liquid as a starting point (adjust for desired thickness).
  • Make a big batch on Sunday for grab-and-go snacks all week.
  • For vegan: Swap Greek yogurt for a high-protein plant yogurt and use plant milk.
