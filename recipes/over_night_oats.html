Over Night Oats

Description

Here's a simple, delicious, and highly-rated Overnight Oats recipe (makes 1 serving).

Ingredients

  • ½ cup (50g) old-fashioned rolled oats (not instant/quick oats)
  • ½ cup (120ml) milk (dairy, almond, oat, soy — any works)
  • ¼ cup (60g) Greek yogurt (optional — makes it creamier and higher in protein)
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds (optional — adds thickness and nutrition)
  • 1–2 teaspoons maple syrup, honey, or sweetener (to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to a jar or container with a lid.
  2. Stir well until everything is combined (no dry clumps).
  3. Seal and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4–6 hours).
  4. In the morning, give it a quick stir. Add a splash of milk if it's too thick.
  5. Enjoy cold, straight from the fridge.

Popular flavour variations

  • Berry Banana (classic): Top with sliced banana + fresh berries + a drizzle of honey.
  • Peanut Butter Chocolate: Add 1 tbsp peanut butter + 1 tbsp cocoa powder + chocolate chips.
  • Apple Cinnamon: Add ½ grated apple + ½ tsp cinnamon + chopped walnuts.
  • Tropical: Add mango + pineapple + shredded coconut.
  • Strawberry Cheesecake: Mix in strawberry jam + extra yogurt on top.

Tips for best results

  • Use rolled oats for the best texture.
  • Ratio: 1:1 oats to liquid is a great starting point — adjust to your preference (thicker = less milk).
  • Make 3–4 jars at once for grab-and-go breakfasts all week.
  • Keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days.
