Tuna Salad

Description

An easy and delicious high protein meal that is also cheap.
There is room to change ingredients and try a range of different flavor combinations.

Ingredients

  • 4 x 95g cans tuna in springwater (drained)
  • 1-2 tbsp Dijon mustard or wholegrain mustard
  • Juice of 1 lemon (or 1-2 tbsp bottled)
  • 1-2 cans chickpeas (drained) — adds bulk & ~15g extra protein
  • 4-6 hard-boiled eggs, chopped (optional)
  • Half red onion diced
  • Half red capsicum diced
  • 1-1.5 cups plain Greek yogurt

Instructions

  1. Drain tuna well
  2. Mix Greek yogurt, mustard, lemon juice, and seasonings in a large bowl.
  3. Add tuna, veggies, pickles, and any boosters. Mix until creamy. Adjust seasoning.
  4. Taste and tweak — more yogurt for creaminess, lemon for brightness.
